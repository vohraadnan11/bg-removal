Background removal runs entirely in-browser via a local ONNX segmentation model (@imgly/background-removal). The first photo you process triggers a one-time model download (roughly 40–80 MB depending on your connection); every photo after that is processed instantly and offline. Large or highly detailed photos take a little longer. This page needs to be served over http/https (e.g. GitHub Pages) — opening it directly as a local file will block the model download.
